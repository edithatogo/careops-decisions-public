# Track 12: AI Transcripts And Minutes

## Goal

Support transcript-assisted minute drafting now and automated Teams transcript ingestion later.

## Scope

- Manual transcript paste/upload MVP.
- AI Builder prompt flow for structured draft minutes.
- Extraction of decisions, actions, risks, attendance references, and unresolved items.
- Later Microsoft Graph transcript retrieval.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AI workflow is optional and logged.
- Draft minutes remain reviewable before approval/finalization.
- Future Graph dependencies are explicit.
