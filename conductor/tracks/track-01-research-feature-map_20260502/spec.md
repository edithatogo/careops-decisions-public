# Track 01: Research Feature Map

## Goal

Research the public Decisions feature surface and map it to an original Power Platform implementation.

## Scope

- Decisions product site, Microsoft Marketplace listing, and Microsoft 365 app certification page.
- Feature grouping across prepare, run, minute, decide, follow through, and report.
- Classify each feature as MVP, Power Platform standard connector, or future Azure/Entra/Graph.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Feature inventory exists under `docs/research`.
- Feature-to-platform map is reflected in architecture and implementation tracks.
