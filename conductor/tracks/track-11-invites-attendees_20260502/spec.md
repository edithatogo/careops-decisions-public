# Track 11: Invites And Attendees

## Goal

Handle meeting invites, attendees, apologies, guests, quorum, and later Teams meeting metadata.

## Scope

- Outlook connector MVP for invite creation/update.
- Attendee and apology records.
- Quorum calculation.
- Later Graph calendar and Teams meeting sync.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Meetings can record invitees, attendance, apologies, and quorum.
- MVP does not require Graph permissions.
