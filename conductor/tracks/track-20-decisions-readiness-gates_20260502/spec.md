# Track 20: Decisions, Licensing, Permissions, And Readiness Gates

## Goal

Make high-impact implementation choices explicit and keep licensing, tenant permissions, and service-account prerequisites visible before live work starts.

## Scope

- Architecture decision records for major choices.
- Licensing and capacity matrix for Power Platform, AI Builder, Power BI/Fabric, Planner, Teams, Outlook, SharePoint, Graph, Azure, and Entra.
- Tenant readiness and permissions matrix for maker, environment admin, Dataverse admin, connection owner, Teams owner, SharePoint owner, Graph admin consent, app registration, and service principals.
- Service account and connection ownership strategy.
- Implementation readiness gates for local, Power Platform development, shared test, and future enterprise environments.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Major decisions have ADRs with status and rationale.
- Licensing and permissions blockers are visible before implementation starts.
- Service account and connection ownership assumptions are explicit and gated.
